1. Introduction

QRNova ("App", "we", "our", or "us") is a QR code and barcode utility that helps users scan codes, generate QR codes, manage local history and favourites, and save, share or print code content.

This Privacy Policy explains how the App accesses, uses, stores and shares information. It also describes diagnostics collected by Google ML Kit.

The App does not require a QRNova account. This notice does not replace any separate permission or consent required by applicable law.

2. Permissions We Use

📷 Camera Permission

Permission used:

  • CAMERA

Purpose:

  • Scan QR codes and barcodes using the device camera.
  • Process camera frames locally for barcode recognition.

📌 QRNova does not upload camera frames to a developer-operated server.

🖼️ Selected Images and Image Saving

Access used:

  • Android system photo picker.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, restricted to Android 9 and earlier.

Purpose:

  • Scan an image that you choose.
  • Save generated PNG images to the shared Pictures folder on older Android versions.
  • Use system storage APIs on newer Android versions.

📌 Photo-picker access is limited to selected images. It does not grant unrestricted access to your photo library.

🌐 Internet & Network Permissions

Permissions declared:

  • INTERNET
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE
  • ACCESS_WIFI_STATE
  • CHANGE_WIFI_STATE

Purpose and current use:

  • Internet/network access can support Google ML Kit diagnostics and online resources.
  • The Wi-Fi-state permissions are declared but are not currently used for an automatic Wi-Fi connection feature.
  • Wi-Fi QR codes use the details you enter or scan.

📌 QRNova does not retrieve saved Wi-Fi passwords or automatically change your network connection.

🔔 Notification Permission

Permission declared:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Current behaviour:

  • This version can request notification permission on Android 13 and later.
  • No system-notification posting feature is implemented in the reviewed version.
  • You may decline this permission; scanning and QR generation do not depend on it.

📌 Permission settings can be managed through Android Settings.

📳 Vibration Permission

Permission used:

  • VIBRATE

Purpose:

  • Provide optional vibration feedback after a successful scan.

📌 Vibration can be disabled in the App's settings.

3. Information We Access

Depending on the features used, the App may process:

  • Camera frames and selected images.
  • Scanned QR code and barcode contents.
  • Text, URLs, phone numbers, email addresses and contact details entered or scanned.
  • Event details and entered or scanned location coordinates.
  • Wi-Fi network names, security settings and passwords contained in codes.
  • UPI IDs, payee names, payment amounts and notes.
  • Saved history, favourites and App preferences.

Successful scans are automatically saved to local history. Generated codes saved through App actions may also appear in history.

📌 This content is used to provide the features selected by the user and is stored locally rather than in a developer-operated cloud database.

4. Information We Do NOT Access or Collect

The reviewed App does not:

  • Read your device's address book, SMS inbox or call logs.
  • Record phone calls or access the microphone.
  • Track your device's GPS location.
  • Retrieve saved Wi-Fi passwords.
  • Collect UPI PINs, bank login credentials or payment-card details.
  • Upload camera frames, selected photo files or saved QR contents to a developer-operated server.

📌 Images, contact details and coordinates that you select, enter or scan can still be processed locally. Google ML Kit diagnostics are described separately below.

5. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

  • Recognise QR codes and barcodes.
  • Generate and display QR codes.
  • Maintain local history, favourites and preferences.
  • Save, copy, share or print selected content.
  • Open supported actions in another app when you request them.
  • Respond to feedback and privacy requests that you send by email.

📌 The App does not use saved QR contents for advertising.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell saved QR content.

When you request an action:

  • Copy places content on the system clipboard.
  • Share passes selected text or a readable image to your chosen app.
  • Export saves an image or document to your chosen destination.
  • Print passes content to Android's print system and your selected print service.
  • Opening a link, payment app, dialer, email, messaging, contacts, calendar or map app passes the relevant content to that app.

These apps and services handle information under their own policies.

📌 Shared or exported codes may contain personal information or credentials. QRNova cannot recall copies already shared with others.

7. Third-Party Services

QRNova uses Google's bundled ML Kit barcode-scanning SDK.

Google documents collection of:

  • Device and application information.
  • Per-installation identifiers.
  • Performance metrics and API configuration.
  • Feature input/output sizes and versions.
  • Feature event types and error codes.

Google uses this information for diagnostics and usage analytics. Google states that the documented telemetry is encrypted in transit using HTTPS and is not transferred to third parties by ML Kit.

This telemetry is separate from the camera frames and images processed locally for scanning.

Further information:

https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure

https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms

https://policies.google.com/privacy

📌 The reviewed App contains no separate advertising or developer analytics SDK. Local scanning does not mean that no SDK telemetry is collected.

8. UPI and Payment-App Actions

QRNova can generate UPI payment QR codes and open a compatible payment app.

  • Payment details come from information entered or scanned by the user.
  • Payment approval and processing happen in the selected payment app.
  • QRNova does not hold funds, collect your UPI PIN, execute a transfer or confirm settlement.
  • A valid-looking QR code does not verify the payee's identity.
  • Verify the payee and amount in your payment app before approving a payment.

📌 Payment disputes, refunds and transaction records are handled by the relevant payment provider or bank. The reviewed version does not implement Google Play Billing or in-app purchases.

9. Data Retention & Deletion

Local history, favourites and preferences remain until you delete them, clear App storage or uninstall the App.

  • Delete an individual history record using its delete control. A temporary Undo action may restore that record if selected.
  • Use the clear-history control in History to remove saved scans, generated records and favourites.
  • Clearing history does not reset preferences or delete exported files.
  • Clear storage/data in Android Settings to remove the local database, preferences and cache.
  • Temporary share images can remain in the App cache until it is cleared.
  • Delete exported PNGs, documents, printed copies, clipboard contents and copies in other apps separately where applicable.

Database deletion removes records from normal App history; it does not guarantee forensic erasure.

Support emails are retained while reasonably needed to respond, maintain necessary support records or meet applicable obligations. You can request deletion of support correspondence by contacting us.

Removing local App data does not automatically erase Google ML Kit telemetry, payment records or data held by another provider.

📌 QRNova has no user accounts. We cannot remotely access or delete your on-device database.

10. Children's Privacy

QRNova is a general-purpose utility and does not ask users to create an age-based profile.

Do not send children's personal information to support unnecessarily. If you believe a child has sent information that should be removed, contact us.

Any use by children must follow applicable law and the App's declared target audience.

11. Data Security

Local history and settings use Android's app-private storage.

  • The local database has no additional App-level encryption.
  • Automatic backup is disabled, and configured backup/device-transfer rules exclude the App database and preferences.
  • Exported or shared copies are outside those exclusions and may be backed up by other services.
  • Google documents HTTPS encryption for ML Kit telemetry.

Use a device lock and protect sensitive exported codes. No storage or transmission system can guarantee absolute security.

📌 You can manage App permissions through Android Settings.
