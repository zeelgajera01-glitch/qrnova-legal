Privacy Policy

Effective / last updated: 12 October 2026 · Developers: Zeel Gajera, Parth Singala, and Kenil SOjitra

This notice explains local QR processing, stored content, Google ML Kit diagnostics and your data choices.

1. Who is responsible

QRNova is developed by Zeel Gajera, Parth Singala, and Kenil SOjitra. For privacy questions or requests, contact zeelgajera010@gmail.com. This policy describes the Android app version distributed with this notice and the accompanying policy pages.

2. What the app does

QRNova scans QR codes and barcodes, generates QR codes, keeps local history and favourites, and lets you copy, export, share or print content. It can pass selected content to a browser, payment app, dialer, email, messaging, contacts, calendar or map app. You do not need to create a QRNova account.

3. Camera and selected images

Camera frames and images you select through the system photo picker are processed on your device for barcode recognition. The app does not upload those frames or image files to a developer-operated server. Accessing a selected photo does not grant the app unrestricted access to your photo library. QR and barcode recognition is separate from the SDK diagnostics described below.

4. Local content and preferences

Successful scans are saved automatically to local history. Generated codes saved or created through app actions can also appear in history. Saved content may include URLs, text, names, contact details, phone numbers, email addresses, event details, coordinates, UPI IDs, payee names, amounts, notes, Wi-Fi network names and passwords. QRNova uses this content to display results, history, favourites and code previews. Appearance, sound and vibration preferences are stored locally. Wi-Fi details come from content you enter or scan; the app does not retrieve your saved Wi-Fi passwords. Location QR codes use entered or scanned coordinates rather than device location tracking.

5. Permissions and device functions

Camera access enables live scanning. Vibration enables optional scan feedback. Internet and network access can support SDK diagnostics and opening online resources; local code recognition and generation can work offline. This build can request notification permission on Android 13 and later; you may decline it, and scanning or generation does not depend on granting it. On Android 9 and earlier, saving a PNG to the shared Pictures folder uses legacy write-storage permission; newer Android versions use system storage APIs. You can change permissions in Android Settings. Contact, calendar, email, dialer and message actions open another app for you to confirm; QRNova does not read your address book, inbox or call log.

6. Google ML Kit diagnostics

QRNova uses the bundled Google ML Kit barcode-scanning SDK. Google documents collection of device and app information, per-installation identifiers, performance metrics, API configuration, input/output sizes, feature versions, event types and error codes for diagnostics and usage analytics. This is SDK telemetry rather than an upload of the photos or camera frames used for scanning. Google states that this telemetry is encrypted in transit using HTTPS and is not transferred to third parties by ML Kit. See https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure, https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms and https://policies.google.com/privacy. QRNova has no separate advertising or developer analytics SDK in the reviewed version. Do not interpret local scanning as a promise that no SDK data is collected.

7. Sharing, payment apps and external services

Copy places selected content on the system clipboard. Export saves a file to your selected destination or the Pictures folder. Share provides text or a readable file to the app you select. Print passes content to the Android print system and your chosen print service. Opening a link or another supported action passes the relevant URI or fields to an external app. Those apps and services may collect or transmit data under their own policies. A UPI action opens a compatible payment app; QRNova does not receive your UPI PIN or bank login credentials, transfer funds, or confirm payment completion. The developer does not sell saved QR contents or upload them to a developer cloud service.

8. Support correspondence and published policy pages

If you email support, the developer receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. They are used to respond, diagnose reported problems and handle privacy requests. Email providers process the correspondence under their own policies. Do not send Wi-Fi passwords, UPI PINs, bank credentials or unnecessary personal details. The accompanying HTML policy files contain no analytics scripts, advertising, cookies or forms. Once hosted, the hosting provider may process ordinary request metadata such as IP address and browser information; its practices depend on the provider selected for publication.

9. Security and backups

History and settings are kept in Android app-private storage. The local database has no additional app-level encryption. The app disables automatic backup and excludes its database, DataStore settings and shared preferences in configured cloud-backup and device-transfer rules. Exported files and copies in other apps are outside these exclusions and may be backed up by those apps or your device services. Use a device lock and share sensitive codes carefully. No storage or transmission system can guarantee absolute security.

10. Retention and deletion

Local history and favourites remain until you remove items, clear history, clear app storage or uninstall the app. Deleting a record removes it from the app history; it is not a promise of forensic erasure. Individual deletion may be undone through the app's temporary Undo action. Clearing history does not reset preferences or delete exported files. Temporary share images can remain in the app cache until Android clears it, you clear cache/storage, or you uninstall. Delete exported images, printed copies, clipboard contents and copies in other apps separately where applicable. The developer retains support correspondence while reasonably needed to resolve the request, maintain necessary support records or meet applicable obligations; you can request deletion at the contact address. Removing local app data does not automatically delete previously collected Google SDK telemetry, which is handled under Google's policies.

11. Your choices and requests

You can decline or revoke permissions, delete local records, clear app storage, and choose whether to export or share. If applicable law gives you rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of information held by the developer, or to receive a copy, contact the address above. The developer may need proportionate verification to protect your information and will handle requests according to applicable law. The developer cannot remotely access or delete your on-device database, third-party payment records, or copies held by another app. You may also contact the relevant data-protection authority where that right applies.

12. Children and policy updates

QRNova is a general-purpose utility and does not ask users to provide an age or create a profile. Do not send children's personal information to support unnecessarily. If you believe a child has sent personal information that should be removed, contact the developer. Any use by children must comply with applicable law and the app's declared audience. This policy will be updated if data practices change; the date above identifies the current text. Material changes will be communicated through an updated notice or release information where appropriate. Privacy disclosures are not a substitute for any separate consent required by law.
