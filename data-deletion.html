Data & Deletion

Effective / last updated: 12 October 2026 · Developer: Zeel Gajera

QRNova has no user accounts. Manage saved codes, exported files and support correspondence using the steps below.

1. No QRNova account

QRNova does not create or maintain user accounts in this version. There is no QRNova account to delete. This page explains how to remove local app data and request deletion of support information. It is a data-management guide, not a remote account-deletion service.

2. Delete one saved code

Open History, use the item's delete control or swipe-to-delete action, and confirm the deletion. A favourite is a saved history record: un-favouriting it only changes its favourite status. To remove the underlying saved content, delete the record. If the temporary Undo message appears, do not select Undo if you intend to keep it deleted.

3. Delete all history

Open History, tap the clear-history control in the top bar, and confirm Delete all. This removes saved scans, generated records and favourites from the app database. It does not reset app preferences, remove exported files or clear copies already shared with other apps.

4. Remove preferences and cached share files

In Android Settings, open Apps > QRNova > Storage (names vary by phone). Clear cache removes temporary cache files; it does not delete saved history. Clear storage or Clear data removes the local database, settings and app cache. Uninstalling removes the app's private local data. Before doing this, export only the records you intend to keep. The app has no developer-operated cloud backup from which these records can be restored.

5. Remove exported or shared copies

Delete saved PNGs from Pictures/QRNova, or from Pictures/QR Saathi if that folder remains from the earlier app name. Delete UPI images or documents from the destination you selected. Remove copies in messaging apps, cloud drives, print services, gallery trash/recycle bins and device backups separately. Clipboard behaviour depends on Android and your keyboard or clipboard service. QRNova cannot recall files already shared or erase another provider's records.

6. Support emails and SDK data

For information you emailed to the developer, contact zeelgajera010@gmail.com with the subject QRNova privacy request. Send the request from the original email address where possible and explain what correspondence you want removed. Do not attach QR contents, credentials or identity documents unnecessarily. Requests are handled according to applicable law, including any necessary verification and retention obligations. Local deletion does not automatically erase Google ML Kit diagnostics; see Google's privacy information at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

7. Deletion limits

Database deletion removes records from QRNova's normal history view; it does not guarantee forensic erasure from storage hardware. Email providers and other services may retain copies under their own rules. Removing QRNova data does not cancel UPI payments, delete bank records, delete contacts or calendar events you saved elsewhere, or change a Wi-Fi password. Contact the relevant service for those actions.
